# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, static output. Deploy target undecided — keep the build host-agnostic.

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating a mid-level (2–5 years) backend engineer: they scan projects, judge depth of technical decisions, then reach out for an interview.
- Freelance clients looking for a backend engineer for outside work: they want proof of shipped systems and a clear way to start a conversation.
- The developer community: peers who arrive via GitHub or social links and stay for the projects and engineering perspective.

## Product Purpose

A personal developer portfolio that presents the owner as a thoughtful mid-level backend engineer. Success means a visitor understands who the owner is, sees two or three real projects with their problem, solution, technical decisions and outcome, and can contact them or reach GitHub/LinkedIn without hunting.

## Positioning

Undecided — depends on the owner's real projects and focus areas, which have not been provided yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: English and Vietnamese, with a visible language switcher and separate URLs per language.
- Sections follow a CV: About, Experience, Projects, Skills, Education, Contact — each on its own page, reached from the overview illustration.
- It is a portfolio, not a game: no gameplay, maps, or quests. One owner-approved exception: the overview is a large pixel scene whose parts are the navigation; clicking one zooms into it and opens that section's page (see CLAUDE.md). Every part keeps a visible text label.
- Important content must never sit behind hidden interactions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no perpetual animation, heavy parallax, or unnecessary WebGL.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual direction "Modern Pixel Editorial" with the dark Minecraft-inspired "diamond pickaxe" palette, pinned in CLAUDE.md.
- Pixel art is original, drawn as SVG pixel grids for this site. The mascot is an original blocky character; no Mojang/Minecraft assets. Its look will be tuned to the owner's description (not yet provided).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. Name, title, links, projects, skills and About copy have not been provided. Every such field ships as a clearly marked placeholder until the owner supplies real content. Do not fabricate projects, metrics, employers, or skills.

## Product Principles

1. Real over impressive: only claims the owner can back with actual work.
2. Scannable first: a recruiter should grasp role, projects and contact within one pass.
3. Decisions over buzzwords: projects explain why technical choices were made.
4. Personality through craft, not gimmicks: pixel art accents the editorial layout, never replaces it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly targets, reduced-motion support (per CLAUDE.md).
